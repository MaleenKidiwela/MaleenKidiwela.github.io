### SeisGo `obsmaster` module (potentially useful)
The `obsmaster` module handles OBS-specific data processing:
- Downloads 4-component OBS data (H1, H2, Z, Pressure)
- Removes instrument response
- Corrects horizontal component orientations
- Computes cross-spectra with QC (flags anomalous windows)
- Calculates transfer functions for tilt and compliance noise removal
- Correction types: ZP (compliance), Z1/Z2-1 (tilt), ZP-21/ZP-H (compliance + tilt)
