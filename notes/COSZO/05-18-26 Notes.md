
## Science Advisory Commitee meeting 


### MEETING OVERVIEW  
This was a Science Advisory Committee (SAC) meeting for the COSZO project, a seafloor geophysical instrumentation initiative connected to the OOI Regional Cabled Array (RCA). The meeting was chaired by William Wilcock, with Anne Sheehan joining late as SAC chair. The meeting lasted approximately 2 hours.

### PROJECT STATUS UPDATE

Deployment Goals  
The project aims to deploy instrumentation at three new sites on the subduction zone margin where there are primary junction boxes on the cabled array but no seafloor geophysical instrumentation. A fourth site on the shelf will receive an additional seismometer package. Together with existing instruments at Slope Base and Hydrate Ridge, this will create a six-node network. A cruise is scheduled for late August with 11 days at sea. Mark Zumberg and Noel Jackson will also redeploy a seafloor optical fiber strain meter on the same cruise.

### Instrument Status

- Broadband seismometer packages (built by Nanometrics): Had problems requiring units to be returned. Three units are back and a fourth is on the way. Huddle tests are just beginning, slightly behind schedule.
- Geodetic and Seismic Sensor Module (GSSM, UW instrument combining A0A with parascientific accelerometer): Testing has gone well. Final testing of internal reservoir is underway and units are nearly ready for deployment.
- Scripps Self-Calibrating Pressure Recorder (SCPR): Was behind schedule but has caught up. Electronics were tested at UW and the instrument is on track for deployment.
- Uncalibrated pressure sensors: In hand.
- Vector current meter: A new model was awaited for a long time and is due to arrive during the current month.
- Connectors: Two challenges identified. ODI connectors were delayed due to a truck accident. In the worst case, one expansion port on one junction box would be unconnected initially, which is considered relatively minor. Dragonfish connectors are on time.
- Seafloor nodes: Three have undergone full testing, two have been pressure tested. Frames are expected to be delivered next month

### DECISIONS

The plan to send all data, including non-Tier 1 instruments, to EarthScope Data Services was confirmed as the preferred approach rather than leaving it only on the PI NAS.

The decision was made that calibrated pressure data will not be served as a derived product directly from EarthScope, but instead a Python package will be made available on GitHub for users to download raw data and apply calibrations themselves.
