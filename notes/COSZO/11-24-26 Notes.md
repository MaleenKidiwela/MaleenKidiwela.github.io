 **Overlap:** all four GSSMs have 7351 files covering the COBSO recording, about 6 days.
 **Rates:** both record at 40 Hz, so we can skip the huge 200 Hz HH files (about 16 GB per channel).

 **COBSO** **health:** median clock quality is 100 on all five units. The BHZ sample counts match their time spans, so there's no sign of gaps. Mass positions 1–3 all have medians within about ±1.2 V, using last time's scaling.

 **Responses:** I found no StationXML with the data, so absolute gain may not be recoverable.

**Large** **offsets:** compared with the logger's filenames, which I assume are true UTC, GSSM01 is about 1h30m ahead during the overlap. GSSM02 and 03 are about 15–35 min ahead.

**GSSM04:** it starts about 24 min ahead, then its clock resets during Nov 11 and reads 2070 through Nov 16.

**Not** **a** **constant** **shift:** the clocks wander and jump. GSSM01 gained 129 s of net label jumps on Nov 12 alone, so one offset per unit won't work.

**Possible** **fix:** on two Nov 12 files (GSSM01 and 02), the line count matches 40 Hz to within about 30 ppm. If the sampling is steady and only the labels are wrong, we can rebuild time from sample count and refine it against COBSO.

**Evidence against it:** GSSM01 on Oct 28 has 0.1% too many lines, and GSSM04 on Nov 11 has 0.6% too few. I've scanned only four files, none from GSSM03.
