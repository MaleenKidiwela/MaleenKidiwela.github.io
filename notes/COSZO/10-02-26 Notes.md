### M2M too slow → found the OOI gold copy

  HYS14 got slow. OOI's queue was clogged by large requests.
- Found OOI's **gold copy** (pre-built NetCDF on `thredds.dataexplorer.oceanobservatories.org`,
  OPeNDAP, no credentials/queue) → reading only `time` + needed variables: **~6–12 s per
  8 Hz day**. Has all 5 velocity streams; **lacks VEL3D-C temperature** → that stays on M2M.
- Built `--source goldcopy` into backfill + investigator; verified identical to M2M results.
- `--workers N` parallel collect; faster M2M polling (5 s, 30 min timeout).
- One-pass mode `backfill --variability`: one pull → MiniSEED + timing CSV row + figure.

### MiniSEED timing problem → "timing" segmenting
- Found the old MiniSEED writer (gap splits only) **misplaced samples**: after an outage,
  burst samples / clock steps shifted the rest of a trace by up to ~2 s (verified end to
  end on HYS14 2016-01-07: 61,700 samples 1.7–1.9 s late). ~40% of sampled days affected.
- New default **timing segmenting**: new trace wherever timestamps leave the regular grid
  by > ½ sample → every sample within ½ sample of OOI's time. Old method kept as `gaps`
  (byte-identical). Applied to backfill and the live pipeline.
- Consequence: the B-station MiniSEED made earlier on the earthnote Mac is wrong-timed →
  regenerated here (don't upload the old ones).

### Moved processing to this Mac + COSZO drive
- Decided to do everything on this Mac; outputs on `/Volumes/COSZO` (29 TB).
- Deployment-start-day fix (first partial day of each deployment was skipped).
- OOI 404 "No data for request" now recognised as a real gap (was treated as a failure).
- Gold-copy files that fail over OPeNDAP (HTTP 500) → full-file download fallback cache.

### Fragmented days → 60 s minimum piece
- AXBA1 2014 days had ~17,000–20,000 gaps/day → 70–80k files per day (~5 million total).
- Tried a day-level rule (median piece < 5 min) → it wrongly dropped a near-complete CZOFF
  day. Final rule (user): **drop traces shorter than 60 s, keep everything ≥ 60 s**
  (max 1,440 files/channel/day). Normal days lose 0–0.75%; AXBA1 shredded days ~all.
  Drops logged in `outlier/dropped_pieces.csv`.
- Clean restart of all 5 stations with all rules (outputs wiped, temperature NetCDF kept).

### OOI double ingestion / foreign records
- CZOFF 2015-10-02..06 had **every record twice** (second copy ~0.03–0.8 s later); later
  periods (CZOFF 2018-09/10, 2019-04; CZSHF 2016-12 → 2019-04) also had **foreign records
  interleaved**. Tell: bad copies have a garbage `internal_timestamp`.
- Fix: on days with > 2% excess samples, drop off-grid records with invalid instrument
  clock (rescue those that fill empty grid slots), then exact duplicates (raw counts /
  instrument clock string). Affected days redone. Also fixed a quadratic slowdown in the
  trace splitter and a 140k-bar histogram that made figures take 15 min.
