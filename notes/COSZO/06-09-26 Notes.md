### Channel & Naming Conventions

- **Location codes:** Current meters → `20`, pressure sensors → `10`, GSSM → `30`, SCPR → `40`, OBS → no location code.
- **Orientation codes:** Use **E, N, Z** instead of 1, 2, Z for velocity components, per Tim and Chad's (EarthScope) recommendation, to stay consistent with COSO data.    
- **Channel codes:** 1 Hz instruments → `L` band (e.g., LOE, LKO for temperature).
- Station names for new Coastal Endurance sites (OSBP, SHBP) to be proposed and confirmed with William.
