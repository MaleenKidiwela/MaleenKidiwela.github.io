## At Sea Blogging 

Blogs are submitted through [https://coszo.org/blog-from-sea.html](https://coszo.org/blog-from-sea.html)

Everyone submitting a blog post will require a Github account

The other materials (Photos, Reports, Daily Plans) can be submitted through google drive [https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1wdKfnJMKEDDmWG26MqVCHkQ-IsvQWchM?usp=drive_link](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1wdKfnJMKEDDmWG26MqVCHkQ-IsvQWchM?usp=drive_link__;!!K-Hz7m0Vt54!i3OQ7bwyuAJ_eC8fZXSEA-s-l0IlmHNZ3uF24LiZycMwX59ncu_R5nIaMiRciH-Mmk5DOiDG7lgVzHo$)

In case Github is not accessible from offshore, please submit word documents to the google drive folder named ‘Blogs’

In case both these methods are not possible, please email these material to [seismic@uw.edu](mailto:seismic@uw.edu)

Once materials are submitted, it will be reviewed and published at [coszo.org](http://coszo.org)
