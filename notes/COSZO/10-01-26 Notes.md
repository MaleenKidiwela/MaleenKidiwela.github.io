
  **1. Pull**

  - **Velocity (all 5 stations), plus B-station temperature:** read straight from OOI's gold copy over OPeNDAP. Only time and the needed variables are

    fetched, into memory, with no NetCDF saved. If OPeNDAP fails on a file, the whole file is downloaded once to a cache and read from there.

  - **C-station temperature:** not in the gold copy, so it comes from M2M. It's requested per day, the NetCDF is saved to the drive, and the saved file

    is then read locally. Days OOI says have "no data" are recorded as gaps; timeouts and errors are retried on the next run.

  

  **2. Deployment:** the deployment for the date is looked up from the param files, including deployments that start partway through the day.

  

  **3. Timing analysis** (same method as the investigator)

  - Fit Δt_true, the real sample interval, by OLS.

  - Find real gaps (genuinely missing samples) and separate them from timestamp jitter.

  **4. MiniSEED trace breaks ("timing" segmenting):** a new trace starts at every real gap, and wherever OOI's timestamps drift more than ½ sample off

  the regular grid (bursts after outages, clock steps). Every sample therefore lands within ½ sample of OOI's recorded time.

  

  **5. Fragmented-day check:** if the day's typical piece is shorter than 5 minutes, no MiniSEED is written, and the day is logged in

  outlier/fragmented_days.csv.

  

  **6. Write MiniSEED:** one file per channel per trace, with sample rate 1/Δt_true, into /Volumes/COSZO/VEL3D/mseed2dmc/<YEAR>/.

  
**7. Timing CSV and figure:** one row per day in output/temporal_anomaly/metrics/<station>_<stream>_variability.csv, plus a 4-panel figure on days with

  gaps. A rerun skips days already in the CSV, so it resumes where it stopped.

  

  **After all runs finish:**

  - **Retry and check:** retry any failed days, then check the logs.

  - **Outliers:** find_dt_true_outliers.py flags days with a bad Δt_true; plot_dt_true_outliers.py lets you review them; segregate_outlier_mseed.py moves

    flagged days to outlier/.

  - **Summary plots:** temporal_anomaly_investigator.py --mode plot.

  - **Upload with dropoff_earthscope.sh:** StationXML first, then MiniSEED with --archive, then status until everything shows ACCEPTED.

  - **Commit:** the regenerated CSVs and figures go into git
