
Today's exploration on the extended catalogues (Southern Vancouver Island 2014–2026, Washington 2007–2026, plus Bostock's 2003–2013 ETS catalogue):

**Setup and checks**

- **Master table and double counting:** built one master table of every Southern Vancouver Island LFE, and confirmed the main pattern survives counting each event once.
- **Washington duplicates:** found and flagged 32 duplicate NW families (catalogue v1.3: about 100 distinct sources). Nothing was deleted.
- **Bostock catalogue:** mapped it to our families. All 115 match, but it covers ETS days only.
- **New inputs:** extracted GPS slip at every family (only a ~35 km blur under the Island), and built hourly tidal stress at every family for 2003–2026.

**Studies and outcomes**

|Study|Result|
|---|---|
|ETS size from GPS|10 of 23 big episodes measurable; slip model OK region-wide only|
|What each ETS did|Fronts at ~5 km/day, half each direction, crossing the border; start points don't repeat|
|Why each ETS differs|How far the front gets explains ~60%; no per-patch slip budget|
|Patch behaviour|Switch on for 2–3 days, then stop abruptly, in hour-long bursts|
|Front band over 23 years|~15 km (Island), ~11 km (Washington), unchanged 2003–2026|
|Tides|Royer 2015 reproduced; response builds over 3–4 days; unchanged over 23 years|
|LFE size|Constant 2014–2026|
|Fixed-station rates (Washington)|Flat 2007–2026; the apparent decline was the station network|
|Between-episode LFEs|Deep, bursty, no warning of the next ETS, in both regions; about half the burst days coincide with nearby tremor bursts|
|Deep quiet after big ETS|1–2 months, provisional; a year-long rebuild was not supported|
|Long record|~14-month cycles, Island and Washington paired, no seasons, no drift|

**Retracted or not supported:** "LFE-rich episodes with no slip" (a labelling error), deep families firing ahead of the front, the quiet month as a firm result, the slow year-long recharge, and LFEs-vs-slip scaling (too few GPS sizes).

**Headline:** the deep fault's ETS behaviour has been the same for two decades. The front band, tidal response, LFE size and rate all agree. Each episode's pattern is set by how far its front travels.
