Everything below is about whether we can see a velocity or structure change at depth (the slow layer above the plate, the plate interface, or the forearc). The time scale is given for each test.

| Test                                                                                  | Result                                                                                                 |

**Instrument checks, needed before any of the above**

- The QC table caught all 6 known problems and found 68 new breaks: clock jumps at B943, B013, GNW and HDW, and gain steps at B009–B011.
- B009's channels are swapped outside 2007–08. This has been fixed, and it barely affected the detections.

**Physics:** ETS strain is about 1e-7, which should change velocity by roughly 1e-4–1e-3 %. That is far below our detection limit of about 0.1 %, so every no-change result above is what we'd expect.
