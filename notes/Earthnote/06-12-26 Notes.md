Tremormetry Methodology had some issues that was first discovered by Fable 5
We were able to fix it. But to start, lets double check the methodology and fix where we need to fix more

The whole analysis is based on the Tremor catalog of PNSN where it has a continuous catalog of Pacific northwest tremor. 
Tremor is a collection of Low Frequency earthquakes. So what we did was to use these windows of tremor to first pick LFEs. 

1. **Pick a station.**
2. **Draw a box** (~100 km) around it.
3. **Pull all PNSN tremor** epicenters in that box, 2010→now. Each tremor entry becomes a short **time window** to inspect.
4. **Run a detector inside those tremor windows** to pick impulsive arrivals. Specifically it's **envelope-peak detection**: bandpass 2–8 Hz → take the Hilbert envelope → find peaks exceeding **3× the local background (SNR ≥ 3)**, with a minimum peak separation. Each surviving peak = one **candidate** arrival.
5. **Cut a waveform snippet at each candidate, cross-correlate all of them, and cluster by shape** (complete-linkage, cc ≥ 0.8). Each cluster = a set of identically-shaped arrivals = one repeating source = a family.
6. **Keep clusters that repeat ≥3 times across ≥3 different years**, average their snippets into a clean **template**, and **densify** = slide that template as a matched filter over the station's **entire continuous record** to log every time it fired.

Two small precisions on your last line:

- The keep-rule is **≥3 members AND ≥3 distinct years** (not just ≥3 repeats) — the years part is what enforces _persistent_ repeaters over transient bursts.
- "Densify" runs over the **full archive**, not just 2010–now. The tremor catalog only located the family; the template then gets matched against everything the station ever recorded (so B018 reaches back to 2006).

Envelope-peak picking is _fine as a band-limited energy-burst triage that bootstraps templates_ — but calling it "picking LFEs" overstates it. It has an impulsive bias, a real discovery-completeness hole, and **no interface/depth or network constraint**, and several of the project's hardest downstream headaches (the spiky-contaminant class, the wrong-depth site-carriers) trace straight back to it. The clustering + matched-filter + trust-battery stack mostly _rescues_ the result after the fact, but the cleaner fix is to push network coincidence and spectral discrimination **upstream into discovery**, not just into the Phase-C densify rebuild.
