
## Phase 3.1 production launch + autoencoder fingerprinting

## Phase 3.1 pipeline design 

Production pipeline `src/phase3p1_pipeline.py` extends Phase 3 with:

- **Denoise gate (band-agnostic)**: compute K=full per-pair stretching, |cc|-weighted combine, then take `mean(|cc_combined|)`. If > 0.8 → keep K=full; if ≤ 0.8 → apply **Wiener soft-thresholding with effective rank K=2.5** (`Σw_i = 2.5`, weights `w_i = σ_i² / (σ_i² + λ²)` with λ chosen by bisection) per pair, re-stretch against the pristine reference, recombine.
- **MAXLAG cut from 60 s to 30 s** (noise window moved from [40, 60] s to [22, 30] s). Saves ~2× disk and memory; adaptive coda is bounded at 20 s so 30 s of lag is plenty.
- **Vectorized stretching** — inner loop swapped so the bulk numpy work is `(n_hours × n_coda)` per trial-ε. ~5× faster on the stretching step.
- **Resume support** via per-station `done.npy` markers; CSV writes incrementally.
- 38 ProcessPoolExecutor workers on cascadia (48 cores), single-thread numpy per worker.

Station selection rule (locked 2026-05-23): one instrument per unique site, priority broadband > short-period > accelerometer. The 304-site list lives at `data/inventory/phase3_priority_2020.csv`.

## Sub-tests done before launch (2026-05-24)

1. **K-sweep on UW.CBS.HH + UW.CBS.EN hourly tensors** with K ∈ [3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 35, 70, 139, 209, 278, 348, 558, 592, 627, 662, 690]. Found a clean inflection at K ≲ 10: |cc| starts rising and dv/v std starts dropping. Higher K values (139–690) are completely flat — the trailing modes carry no signal AND no noise that the PWS step hasn't already concentrated. See `data/phase13/svd_sweeps/svd_sweep.png` (log–log).
2. **K=2 vs Wiener eff_K=2.0 vs K=full overlay** — K=2 over-truncates the broadband (preserves dv/v shape but amplitude clipped to ~60 % of true), Wiener eff_K=2.0 even more aggressive. Hard K=2 and Wiener at eff_K=2 are nearly identical on the accelerometer.
3. **K=3 dv/v vs K=full time series** — UW.CBS.HH dv/v amplitudes preserved at K=3, ZN collapses (std crashes to 0.015 %); UW.CBS.EN dv/v *recovered* a coherent ~±0.05 % signal from K=full's ±2 % noise.
4. **Wiener eff_K=2.5 vs hard K=3 vs K=full** (`data/phase13/svd_sweeps/wiener_vs_hard3.png`) — Wiener 2.5 over-shrinks broadband amplitude, but on accelerometer it gives a smooth, coherent dv/v series with |cc| roughly doubled.

Net decision: **Wiener eff_K=2.5 with pristine reference** is the denoise rule when |cc|_full ≤ 0.8. See `~/.claude/projects/-home-seismic/memory/earthnote_phase3_svd_denoise.md`.

## Smoke test then launch

- **UW.MDW Jan 2020**, single worker, `--max-doys 31`: 50 min wall (vs 67–69 min for previous Phase 3 smoke tests on CBS.HH/CBS.EN — confirms ~25 % speedup from MAXLAG=30 + vectorize). K=full combined |cc|=0.963 → gate passed, no denoise. Adaptive coda fired: [1.50, 4.60] s. **First time the intersect-three-pairs adaptive rule succeeded in Phase 3** (CBS.HH and CBS.EN both fell back to default).
- **Launched 110 broadband full year 2020** at 08:25, 38 workers. Status as of evening: 67–86 of 110 done (61–78 %), 0 errors, mean per-station elapsed ~9.9 h.

## Surprising finding — most broadband stations need denoise at year scale

| Metric | K=full | Post-Wiener |
|---|---|---|
| Median combined |cc| | **0.54** | **0.85** |
| Min combined |cc| | 0.38 | 0.60 |
| Max combined |cc| | 0.80 | 0.96 |
| denoise_applied = True | — | 64 / 67 (95 %) |

UW.MDW Jan-only was |cc|=0.96 well above the 0.8 gate. Same station on full year drops because the year-long PWS reference averages real dv/v drift — no individual hour matches the year-averaged shape perfectly, so |cc| against the year ref is necessarily lower than against a 1-month ref. Conclusion: the 0.8 gate is operating correctly; **Wiener denoise is the dominant path for year-scale production**, not the exception.

Three stations passed the gate (|cc|_full > 0.8). Need to look at them once the run finishes to see what makes them special.

## Plotting

`src/phase3p1_plot.py` generates per-station 4×2 diagnostic plots from `stacks_3p1/{tag}/`. Iterated through three improvements today:

1. **y-axis of hourly dv/v fixed to ±1 %** (was auto-scaled — combined series got dwarfed by per-pair outliers).
2. **Per-pair lines at 40 % transparency** so the combined dv/v line dominates.
3. **CC sections row-normalized to absmax** — each hour scales to its own peak amplitude, making faint and loud hours equally readable. Coda banding is now clearly visible across the year.

One-off override: `--dvv-ylim 0.1` for stations with very low std (e.g. CC.SHRK).

## Autoencoder fingerprinting on CC.SHRK (today's experiment)

Installed PyTorch CPU + scikit-learn + UMAP into the `noisepy2` env. Wrote `src/cc_shrk_fingerprint.py`:

- Loaded `CC_SHRK_X_BH` hourly tensors (8737 hours × 3 pairs × 1201 lag samples after zoom to ±15 s).
- Per-hour normalized each row to absmax.
- 1-D conv autoencoder: 3 channels → Conv1D(32, 64, 128) with strided convs → AdaptiveAvgPool to 8 → Linear bottleneck of dim 32 → ConvTranspose1d mirror. 179,619 params.
- Trained 80 epochs on CPU (4 threads, so it didn't compete with the pipeline). 436 s wall. Loss converged 0.0034 → 0.00186.
- Extracted 32-D fingerprint per hour → UMAP to 2-D → KMeans k=6.

UMAP scatter shows long curve-like trails — adjacent-in-time hours are nearby in fingerprint space, confirming temporal continuity. Cluster mean CCs look very similar (because absmax normalization clamps the zero-lag peak to ±1; real differences are subtler coda variations not visible at the y-scale).

**Per-month top cluster** (`cc_shrk_monthly_cluster.py`, `monthly_top_cluster.png`):

Next steps if we iterate:
- Plot cluster mean *deviation* from global mean (subtle shape diffs invisible at raw scale).
- Train autoencoder on coda-only region (exclude zero-lag peak).
- Try HDBSCAN to auto-detect cluster count.
- Apply to other stations and see if the seasonal cluster pattern generalizes.
