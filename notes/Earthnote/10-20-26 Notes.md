## Polarization method

The polarization method is covariance/eigenvector analysis, followed by a bootstrap comparison across time.

For each family, we use Z, N and E together to measure:

- **Orientation:** the direction of the particle motion.
- **Shape:** whether that motion is mostly linear or spreads through a plane.
- **Component balance:** how much motion is horizontal versus vertical.

We compare these measurements between time periods and repeatedly resample independent time blocks to estimate uncertainty. A stability test then asks whether the largest plausible change is smaller than a specified tolerance. Simply finding no statistically significant change is insufficient to establish stability.
