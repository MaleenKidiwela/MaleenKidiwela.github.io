## Tremorferometry second route
## Phase 1 — Set up ground truth and data

1. **Reframed the problem.** The original discovery (envelope-peak picks in tremor windows → cluster → densify) is single-component, 2-s, cc≥0.8, and admits noise. Goal: identify genuine LFEs by _waveform features_ and reject noise/earthquakes/blasts/cultural — toward an EQTransformer-style picker and cleaner discovery seeds.
2. **Chose ground truth + stations.** Used the **Lin (2023) LFE catalog** as labeled positives; worked at the Lin-region stations **PGC** (broadband) and **B011 / B926** (boreholes), with **B011** primary (it's a borehole like the deployment targets, in the dense part of Lin's catalog). Downloaded 3-component data for these.
3. **Defined 23 hand-crafted features** (`feature_defs.py`) on 40-s windows: spectral shape (slope, centroid, band fractions, HF ratio), envelope/onset (kurtosis, crest, rise-time), and **3-component polarization** (H/V, rectilinearity, planarity).

## Phase 2 — Build labeled examples

4. **Positives:** curated Lin LFEs (N≥6 stations, residual<1.2 s, ≤35 km) → windows (`extract_lin_positives.py`).
5. **Negatives, in stages:** random-noise windows (RAND), **ANSS earthquakes** (`extract_eq_negatives.py`), **ANSS explosions/blasts** (same script, `--eventtype explosion`), and **in-tremor ambient/cultural** windows (`extract_tremor_noise.py`).

## Phase 3 — Prove the features identify LFEs

6. **LFE vs noise** (`analyze_lfe_features.py`): CV-AUC **0.95–0.98** at both PGC and B011, and — key control — **unchanged when all amplitude/SNR features are removed** → it's waveform _character_, not loudness. Top features: spectral slope, S-polarization, HF depletion, band concentration.
7. **LFE vs earthquakes vs blasts** (`analyze_multiclass.py`): **0.97** each — it rejects the impulsive confusers. The one hard residual is **in-tremor ambient noise (0.81)**, which shares the LFE low-frequency band.
8. **Cross-checked against the project's existing families** (`family_stack_test.py`): coherently-stacked B011 families are spectrally LFE-consistent (this is where I caught and corrected 3 of my own method artifacts).

## Phase 4 — Build the detectors/pickers

9. **Per-window picker v0** (`build_picker_v0.py`): calibrated 3-class LFE/EQ/NOISE classifier, **LFE AUC 0.975**, saved model. Dominant feature: `hv_ratio` (S-polarization).
10. **Transfer test** (`build_picker_v0.py --sta PGC`): holds on the broadband instrument (0.97) → not a single-instrument artifact.
11. **Unsupervised autoencoder** (`extract_spectrograms.py` + `train_autoencoder.py`): a conv-AE on spectrograms rediscovers the LFE cluster with **no labels** (latent AUC 0.965) — independent confirmation.
12. **Per-sample U-Net picker** (`build_picker_dataset.py` + `train_phasenet.py`): the actual EQTransformer-style continuous P(LFE) stream — works (per-sample AUC 0.85) but is **single-station-capped** (precise-label refinement failed because LFEs are patch-specific + emergent).
13. **Continuous scanner** (`scan_continuous.py`): deployed picker v0 over whole days → strong **LFE-activity detector** (ETS day P=0.94 vs quiet day 0.05).

## Phase 5 — Apply it to discovery (the goal: clean LFE picking in tremor windows)

14. **Tremor-window picker** (`build_tremor_picker.py`): trained LFE vs {EQ, blast, in-tremor noise}; rejects EQ/blast at 0.97, enriches LFEs ~3×.
15. **Wired into real GPU discovery** (`score_candidates.py` → `discover_gpu` → `compare_discovery.py`): scored 415k B011 candidates, ran clustering on the full vs P(LFE)-filtered pool. Learned to **filter at the family level, not per-candidate**; and that **B011's contamination is in-tremor noise, not EQ/blast** (so the picker bites harder at EQ/blast-contaminated stations).
16. **Template-length test** (`discovery_filter_demo.py` / `discover_gpu` runs): tested 2 s vs 4 s vs 6 s templates — **longer does _not_ help clustering** (LFE coda decorrelates; ~2 s ≈ the coherent duration). Surfaced that the whole discovery/clustering backbone is **vertical-only**, and the strongest discriminator (S-polarization) is invisible to it — making **3-component clustering** the most promising untested next lever.

## Net outcome

A **credible, physical, validated LFE fingerprint** (separates LFEs from noise _and_ earthquakes/blasts at ~0.97–0.98, SNR-independent, instrument-transferable, autoencoder-confirmed); a **deployable per-window/family-level LFE detector**; an **EQTransformer-style per-sample picker** whose single-station ceiling is characterized; and the honest conclusion that the next real gains are **3-component clustering** and **multi-station (network)** input.
