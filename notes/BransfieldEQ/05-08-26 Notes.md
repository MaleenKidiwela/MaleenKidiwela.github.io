## Findings worth remembering

These are now in [[01-ml-picking]] but worth surfacing here as the day's "discoveries":

1. **ZX OBS use low-gain seismometer code** (`?L?`), not high-gain (`?H?`). Initial channel filter `EH?,HH?,BH?,SH?` returned zero for ZX. Real channels: `ELZ` @ 200 Hz vertical, `SL1`/`SL2` @ 100 Hz horizontals, `EDH` @ 200 Hz hydrophone.
2. **ZX is a reused temp network code** spanning 2003–2022 across multiple deployments (Bransfield 2019, New Zealand 2021/2022, China, UK, Costa Rica). Filter to `BRA*` station glob + 2019-01 → 2020-03 window.
3. **5M is also reused** — bbox filter to Bransfield, since the same code includes European stations.
4. **R4DE2 epoch trap** — the same Raspberry Shake station code has bounced between Uruguay and Bransfield several times. FDSN bbox filter at the channel-epoch level (Raspberry Shake supports it) drops the Uruguay epochs cleanly. Within our window, only ~5 days of true Bransfield data; after 2020-08 it's continuous through 2025 if we ever extend the window.
5. **OBS water depth comes free from StationXML** — `elevation` field is negative for seafloor sensors. GEBCO not needed for Stage 1; only for 3D ray paths in Stage 4.
6. **Mixed sample rates within a single ZX station** (200 Hz vertical, 100 Hz horizontals). Picker resamples Z to 100 Hz before PhaseNet.

## Stage 3 + 4 stack locked

Discussed associators and locators in detail:

- **Associator: PyOcto** (Münchmeyer, same author as SeisBench). SeisBench itself doesn't include an associator; PyOcto is the natural pairing — fast octree search, robust to ML pick noise, drops in directly. GaMMA reserved for a 1-week sanity cross-check.
- **Locator stack: PyOcto → NLLoc → waveform CC → GrowClust → tomoDD (optional)**. NLLoc gives PDFs (essential for our sparse, azimuthally biased network). GrowClust chosen over HypoDD as primary DD because median-based solver is more robust to ML pick outliers. tomoDD on a curated subset for cross-validation.
- **Velocity model: user has an Orca 3D blended with a 1D background, and an existing NLLoc setup using it.** Bring it in at Stage 4 — do not rewrite. Vp only; will use Vp/Vs ≈ 1.78 for S travel-times. User will provide the 1D background model file.
- **Run the full Stage 3-4 stack twice** — once for tectonic, once for icequake — with different velocity priors and depth ranges.
- **Cross-correlation step** between NLLoc and GrowClust is the biggest precision lever (catalog-only DD: ~100 m, CC-DD: ~10 m). Use EQcorrscan or similar.

## PhaseNet caught a missed event at 23:51:34 — validation win

While inspecting Dec 26 results, a 4-station cluster surfaced at **23:51:34–36**, ~24 s after manual event 2 (23:51:10). PhaseNet picks at BRA19/BRA21/BRA22/BRA20 with S-P times of 0.6–0.8 s on the cleanest stations — **a real, second local seismic event the manual analyst either missed or merged with event 2**. Not coda of event 2 (24 s gap is far too long for a local source).

> [!warning] Don't call this an "aftershock" yet
> Earlier draft labeled this as an aftershock — that overclaimed. We don't have magnitudes or locations yet, so the physical relationship between the two events is unconstrained. Better-supported labels for a Bransfield rift+volcanic context:
> - **Earthquake swarm pair** (most likely; characteristic of spreading axes and volcanic regions)
> - Doublet/multiplet on the same fault patch
> - Triggered seismicity (static or dynamic stress transfer)
> - Aftershock *if* event 1 turns out to be larger
> - Foreshock–mainshock *if* event 2 is larger
>
> Pinning it down needs Stage 4 magnitudes + locations + waveform CC between the two.

Notes:
- Borderline confidence under `instance` weights (BRA19 P prob 0.34 vs 0.97 with `stead`). Suggests an ensemble may help on marginal events.
- 4 stations is right at PyOcto's typical association minimum — won't be lost downstream.
- This is exactly the upside of running an ML picker over the full record: catalog-completeness gain, not just QA against the existing catalog.
- Captured in [[01-ml-picking]] under "PhaseNet caught a missed event at +25 s".

## Afternoon testing — threshold + model comparison + paper review

### Threshold 0.1 sweep on PhaseNet
Re-ran Dec 26 picks with `instance @ 0.1` (down from 0.2):

| Setting | ML picks | P recall | S recall | P prec (EW) | S prec (EW) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `stead` @ 0.3 | 2,068 | 9% | 17% | 33% | 33% |
| `instance` @ 0.2 | 3,307 | 45% | 42% | 38% | 63% |
| **`instance` @ 0.1** | **6,902** | **64%** | 42% | **47%** | 63% |

P recall jumped 45 → 64 % with precision *also* up (rare double win). S recall flat — bounded by something other than threshold (likely OBS horizontals being noisier on small events). **`instance @ 0.1` locked as the new default.** Defaults updated in `03_run_phasenet.py` and `slurm/pick.sbatch`.

### EQTransformer comparison
Ran EQT @ instance @ 0.1 on Dec 26 (same data, separate output dir `catalogs/picks_eqt_instance/`):

| Model | Total picks | P | S | P recall | S recall | P prec (EW) | S prec (EW) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PhaseNet** | 6,902 | 5,809 | 1,093 | **64%** | 42% | **47%** | 63% |
| **EQTransformer** | 7,126 | 5,939 | 1,187 | 36% | **50%** | 31% | **67%** |

**Mixed result, complementary strengths:**
- PhaseNet wins on P (U-Net excels at sharp impulsive arrivals).
- EQT wins on S (transformer + LSTM better for variable-character S onsets).

Recommendation: run **both** on the cluster (cheap on GPU); union of picks → PyOcto handles overlap during association. Coded `--model` and `--out-subdir` flags in `03_run_phasenet.py` to support side-by-side runs.

### Wang et al. 2024 SRL paper review — Axial Seamount

Read [[srl-2024086.1.pdf]] (Wang, Waldhauser, Schaff, Tolstoy, Wilcock, Tan — "Real-Time Detection of Volcanic Unrest and Eruption at Axial Seamount Using Machine Learning"). Their stack:

```
PhaseNet → GaMMA → NLLoc → HypoDD + waveform CC → SpecUFEx (unsupervised classification)
+ 30-50/4-50 Hz freq-ratio rule for fin-whale calls
+ 15s overlapping windows in real-time (6s during high seismicity)
```

**Validates our locked stack** almost component-for-component. Things to adopt:
- **SpecUFEx** unsupervised feature clustering for [[02-discrimination]] — alternative or complement to our planned supervised CNN.
- **Whale-call frequency ratio rule** (30–50 / 4–50 Hz energy) — drop-in deterministic discriminator.
- **1 s pre-P + 3 s post-P spectrogram window** for classification features — adopt as default.
- **PhaseNet pick std on OBS**: 17 ms (P), 40/28 ms (S) — useful prior for our CC differential-time uncertainty.
- **Self-training (Yarowsky) for label reconciliation** across stations — useful pattern for class-aware FP attribution.

What we do that they don't: explicit hydrophone features, manual analyst picks as direct ground truth. Their training data was Kurtosis-picked baseline.
