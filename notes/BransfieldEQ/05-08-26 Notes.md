## Findings worth remembering

These are now in [[01-ml-picking]] but worth surfacing here as the day's "discoveries":

1. **ZX OBS use low-gain seismometer code** (`?L?`), not high-gain (`?H?`). Initial channel filter `EH?,HH?,BH?,SH?` returned zero for ZX. Real channels: `ELZ` @ 200 Hz vertical, `SL1`/`SL2` @ 100 Hz horizontals, `EDH` @ 200 Hz hydrophone.
2. **ZX is a reused temp network code** spanning 2003–2022 across multiple deployments (Bransfield 2019, New Zealand 2021/2022, China, UK, Costa Rica). Filter to `BRA*` station glob + 2019-01 → 2020-03 window.
3. **5M is also reused** — bbox filter to Bransfield, since the same code includes European stations.
4. **R4DE2 epoch trap** — the same Raspberry Shake station code has bounced between Uruguay and Bransfield several times. FDSN bbox filter at the channel-epoch level (Raspberry Shake supports it) drops the Uruguay epochs cleanly. Within our window, only ~5 days of true Bransfield data; after 2020-08 it's continuous through 2025 if we ever extend the window.
5. **OBS water depth comes free from StationXML** — `elevation` field is negative for seafloor sensors. GEBCO not needed for Stage 1; only for 3D ray paths in Stage 4.
6. **Mixed sample rates within a single ZX station** (200 Hz vertical, 100 Hz horizontals). Picker resamples Z to 100 Hz before PhaseNet.

## Validation pipeline ready

Built the missing pieces so PhaseNet output can be measured against manual ground truth as soon as it exists:

- `manual_picks.resolve_networks()` joins picks against `station_geometry.csv` to fill in network codes (NLLoc/Nordic don't carry them). 58,606 / 58,630 picks resolved; only `ESPZ` + `OHG` (24 picks, Antarctic regional stations outside our targets) stay blank.
- `scripts/05_validate_picks.py` — greedy nearest-time matcher. For each (network, station, phase) reports TP/FP/FN, precision, recall, F1, residual mean/std. Defaults: P ±0.5 s, S ±1.0 s. Writes `catalogs/validation_report.csv` (per net × sta × phase) and `catalogs/validation_per_pick.csv` (per-pick, including residuals). Handles empty PhaseNet directory.

## Stage 3 + 4 stack locked

Discussed associators and locators in detail:

- **Associator: PyOcto** (Münchmeyer, same author as SeisBench). SeisBench itself doesn't include an associator; PyOcto is the natural pairing — fast octree search, robust to ML pick noise, drops in directly. GaMMA reserved for a 1-week sanity cross-check.
- **Locator stack: PyOcto → NLLoc → waveform CC → GrowClust → tomoDD (optional)**. NLLoc gives PDFs (essential for our sparse, azimuthally biased network). GrowClust chosen over HypoDD as primary DD because median-based solver is more robust to ML pick outliers. tomoDD on a curated subset for cross-validation.
- **Velocity model: user has an Orca 3D blended with a 1D background, and an existing NLLoc setup using it.** Bring it in at Stage 4 — do not rewrite. Vp only; will use Vp/Vs ≈ 1.78 for S travel-times. User will provide the 1D background model file.
- **Run the full Stage 3-4 stack twice** — once for tectonic, once for icequake — with different velocity priors and depth ranges.
- **Cross-correlation step** between NLLoc and GrowClust is the biggest precision lever (catalog-only DD: ~100 m, CC-DD: ~10 m). Use EQcorrscan or similar.

## End-to-end test on 2019-12-26

Ran the full Stage 1 workflow on one day to validate before cluster commitment.

**Download** (`02_download_waveforms.py --start 2019-12-26 --end 2019-12-27`):
- 35 / 37 stations returned data (R4DE2 correctly excluded — Uruguay epoch on this date).
- 1.66 GB total (~2× initial estimate; reasonable with hydrophone + GEOFON OBS included).

**Picking** (`03_run_phasenet.py`):
- CPU on M-series Mac, **17 min** for 35 station-days.
- Peak RAM **3.1 GB** (PhaseNet activations + ObsPy stream).
- Wall time scales linearly: full year × 38 stations ≈ 135 hours sequential, ~17 hours with an 8-way SLURM array, ~2 hours with GPU.

**Validation against manual picks** (Dec 26 has 23 unique manual picks across 2 events; required dedup since `mag07` ⊂ `magall` was double-counting):

| Setting | Stations w/ picks | ML picks | P recall | S recall | P prec (event-window) | S prec (event-window) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `stead` @ 0.3 | 32 | 2,068 | 9% (1/11) | 17% (2/12) | 33% | 33% |
| **`instance` @ 0.2** | 35 | 3,307 | **45%** (5/11) | **42%** (5/12) | **38%** | **63%** |

Both recall AND precision up — the rare double win. Default switched in `03_run_phasenet.py` and `slurm/pick.sbatch` to `instance` weights @ threshold 0.2. STEAD is land-only training data; `instance` was trained on European OBS, much closer to BRAVOSEIS domain.

Why recall isn't higher yet: PhaseNet still picks events on a *different subset of stations* than the manual analyst — for the 13:43:42 event, manual used BRA14/16/19/20/21/22/25 and PhaseNet used BRA05/13/14/15/16/18. Only BRA14 + BRA16 overlap. Doesn't matter for **association** (PyOcto only needs ≥4 stations), but limits per-station validation. Future fine-tuning on Bransfield manual picks should close more of the gap.

## PhaseNet caught a missed event at 23:51:34 — validation win

While inspecting Dec 26 results, a 4-station cluster surfaced at **23:51:34–36**, ~25 s after manual event 2 (23:51:10). PhaseNet picks at BRA19/BRA21/BRA22/BRA20 with S-P times of 0.6–0.8 s on the cleanest stations — **a real, second local earthquake the manual analyst either missed or merged with event 2**. Not coda of event 2 (24 s is far too long for a local source).

Notes:
- Borderline confidence under `instance` weights (BRA19 P prob 0.34 vs 0.97 with `stead`). Suggests an ensemble may help on marginal events.
- 4 stations is right at PyOcto's typical association minimum — won't be lost downstream.
- This is exactly the upside of running an ML picker over the full record: catalog-completeness gain, not just QA against the existing catalog.
- Captured in [[01-ml-picking]] under "PhaseNet caught a missed event at +25 s".
